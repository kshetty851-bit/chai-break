About the developer

Karthik Shetty · Chai Break Studios

I'm an indie developer making small, cheerful games about everyday Indian life: gully cricket with a tyre for stumps, the chai stall at the corner, the Mumbai local, the mom with a chappal in her hand. Each one is meant to be picked up in a chai break and put down with a smile.

Chai Break Studios is just me, building fast with Claude as my co-developer: 19 games so far, and the first ones are on their way to Google Play.

Want to test a game before it launches? Write to me at kshetty851@gmail.com.
