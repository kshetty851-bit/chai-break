Dev log 💀 (one-man army, zero chill)

Karthik Shetty · CEO, CTO, intern, QA, chai-wala and emotional support human of Chai Break Studios

20 games1 human∞ cutting chai0 sleep3 braincells (shared)

Hi, I'm Karthik. I make tiny games about desi life: gully cricket, the chai tapri, the Mumbai local, and Mummy's chappal, which has a 100% hit rate and zero cooldown. If you survived one, congrats bestie, you've been beta-testing since birth.

Chai Break Studios is me, one overheating laptop and Claude as my co-dev. We shipped 20 games in a few weeks. Flex or cry for help? Yes. My sleep schedule flatlined in week one and nobody showed up to the funeral. Not even me. I was debugging.

Currently in my Google Play review era: submit, refresh, stare at the wall, refresh again. The 14-day tester rule is my villain origin story and I'm still stuck in act one. If a game crashes, no it didn't, that's lore.

Hobbies include arguing with a compiler at 4am and calling it self-care. My will is sorted: the chai goes to the testers, the bugs go to whoever taps "report". The obituary will read: "died doing what he loved: fixing one typo." Delulu? Maybe. Shipping? Always.
