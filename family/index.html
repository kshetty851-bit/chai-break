        How the Play connection works

        A script on the studio Mac, play_sync.py, signs in to Google Play with a read-only service account each morning and saves a snapshot: tracks and release status, recent reviews and ratings, crash and ANR rates, and whether the public store page exists. This page shows only that snapshot. The key stays on the Mac and is never part of this page.

        Play's API does not say "in review", so review status comes from the release notes until the store page goes public. Install counts come from Play's monthly reports and are not connected yet.
